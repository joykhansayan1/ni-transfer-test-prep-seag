Restore purchases

On the paywall or in Settings, tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple Account used to buy. On Android, sign in to Google Play with the Google account used to buy, then tap Restore Purchases.

Manage subscription

Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscription in the App Settings.

On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or use Manage Subscription in the App Settings.

Parental gate

Purchases, restores and links that leave the App ask a grown-up to answer a multiplication question first, so children cannot buy or browse on their own.
