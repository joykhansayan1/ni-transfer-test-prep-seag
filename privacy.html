Privacy Policy

NI Transfer Test Prep (SEAG) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NI Transfer Test Prep (SEAG) (the “App”, on iPhone and iPad from the App Store and on Android from Google Play), published by MD Shahariar Nazim Joy (“we”), handles information. The App is private by design.

1. We do not collect personal data

The App does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There are no accounts or logins. There is no third-party analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

2. Data stored on your device

Study progress (answers, streak, bookmarks, mock history, topic mastery, preferences, test date) is stored only on your device (Apple on-device storage on iOS; the app’s private storage on Android). It never leaves your device and is removed if you delete the App.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. We never see payment card details. Apple’s handling of purchase data is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

On Android, purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see payment card details. Google’s handling of purchase data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The App only asks Google Play whether a purchase is active, so it can unlock content on your device.

4. Notifications

If you enable the optional daily study reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device only. Disable anytime in the App or in iOS Settings. On Android 13 and later the App asks for notification permission only when you turn the reminder on; disable it in the App or in Android Settings → Apps → Notifications.

5. No tracking

We do not track you across apps or websites and do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA on iOS, Android Advertising ID on Android; the Android app does not request the advertising ID permission). The App does not require Camera, Photos, Contacts, Location, Microphone, or Health access.

6. Children

The App provides educational practice for children preparing for NI transfer-style assessments, typically used with a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Apple Account holders manage purchases.

On Android, the App is built for Google Play’s Families policy: it contains no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data, and a parental gate (a question for a grown-up) protects every purchase, restore and link that leaves the App. The Google account holder manages purchases.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted on this page with a new “last updated” date.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: dmjks120@gmail.com.

This App is an independent study aid and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to SEAG, GL Assessment, any grammar school, or Education Authority NI.
